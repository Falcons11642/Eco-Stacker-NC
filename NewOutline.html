Eco-Stacker ⛰️

Simulate moving river rocks in North Carolina
Environmental Impact:

The river is peaceful. Rocks are safely on the ground sheltering tiny creatures.

Ecosystem Health: 100% | Rocks: 0/5 | Time: 0 days
NC River Creatures Affected:
🦎
Eastern Hellbender

North America's largest salamander, native to NC hills. They rely on massive flat base rocks to hide and protect their nests.

🐛
Mayfly Nymph

Found under rocks in pristine Wilkes County streams. They use flat bodies to anchor themselves against fast water currents.

🐟
Carolina Darter

A small, rare fish that lives strictly at the bottom of clean Piedmont streams, hiding from predators behind gravel patches.

🎣
Brook Trout

NC's only native trout. They lay eggs in the clean, sediment-free gravel spaces kept safe by undisturbed large river rocks.
